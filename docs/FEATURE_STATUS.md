# Feature status and evidence

This page describes the current source rather than the historical audit reports. “Synthetic browser” means a local HTTPS fixture in Chromium with a fake camera and synthetic screen-share track; it does **not** mean a real conferencing site or physical hardware was tested.

| Area | Implemented behavior | Evidence and limitations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting targeting | Active supported tab, or the only supported tab; ambiguous multi-tab actions are refused. Emergency Restore broadcasts and reports incomplete delivery. | Worker/popup tests and synthetic browser exercise routing; actual meeting-site behavior is unverified. |
| Camera sender | Tracks camera provenance observed through `getUserMedia`, requires a matching self-preview, and transactionally replaces/restores known WebRTC video senders. Unknown senders, remote media and screen shares are left alone. | Bridge unit tests cover rollback and races; synthetic Chromium exercises a fake camera and screen-share sender. Hidden/transformed previews, wrappers, pre-injection tracks and non-WebRTC pipelines may be unsupported. |
| Local tile overlay | Renders over a verified, usable self-preview when outgoing replacement is unavailable. | Synthetic Chromium checks overlay pixels. This is local-only and may itself be unavailable; it does not affect what participants receive. |
| Emergency restore / mute | Attempts restoration, reports failure, and never automatically unmutes. Automatic mute and mute-only gestures act only on a recognizable, unambiguous meeting microphone control; mute-only does nothing if already muted. | Mock tests cover mute ownership and failure. Real meeting controls, localizations and disconnects need manual validation. |
| In-call background plate | Explicit five-second capture, preview, approval and clear; otherwise a solid fallback is used. A captured plate stays in tab memory and is invalidated on detected camera change. | Mock-based capture/review tests. The extension cannot verify that a frame is empty, and an approved plate can be sent in meeting video. |
| Automatic detection | Local MediaPipe inference; Smart requires audio confirmation by default and fails closed if required audio is unavailable. Gesture-only and Manual do not open the detector microphone; sound alone cannot trigger. Configured action mapping is honored in calls. | Synthetic landmarks/audio tests, lifecycle tests, and actual model initialization/inference on a fake camera in Chromium. Physical snap accuracy, latency and false-trigger rate are unmeasured. |
| CSP fallback | If in-page WASM initialization fails, per-tab models run in an extension-origin offscreen document. Transient JPEG frames are capped to a 256-pixel longest edge and eight requests/s; four models maximum. | Actual inference and cleanup tested with synthetic `script-src 'none'` in Chromium 150. Other CSPs, GPUs, Chrome 121 and real sites are not validated. Compression/sampling can miss gestures. |
| Studio and calibration | Local preview, particle effects, Studio-only background reconstruction and a separate multi-phase gesture detector. Camera/model requests are cancelled on stop/unmount; Studio preview is not the meeting output. | DOM and lifecycle tests plus a standalone Chromium fake-camera model smoke. Live physical calibration, segmentation quality and OBS capture are unverified. |
| Settings and controls | Canonical synchronized settings are sanitized and serialized; popup and Studio expose status and failures. Some settings are Studio-specific, including camera/device selection, smoothing and background method. | Unit tests cover migrations, concurrent writes, failed storage and routing. `chrome.storage.sync` may synchronize preferences via Chrome. |
| Build and package | `npm test` does not require a generated zip. `npm run verify` typechecks, builds, checks archive integrity/content, and runs tests. | Run the commands locally for current results; no fixed historical test count or package size is a guarantee. Packaging currently needs `zip`/`unzip`. |

## Unverified or conditional

- No authenticated Google Meet, Teams or Zoom session, physical camera/microphone, browser-version matrix, accessibility audit with assistive technology, or OBS workflow was exercised here.
- A successful sender replacement is not proof of the view at a remote participant. Verify the actual meeting video and microphone with another participant.
- The Studio and meeting detector/renderer are separate implementations. Settings shared through storage do not imply identical effects, background reconstruction, timing, or detector accuracy.
- The extension cannot guarantee a restore when a track ends or a site rejects replacement. Camera/microphone safety controls in the meeting remain authoritative.
- The legacy `strictLocalOnly` and `autoStartOnCall` preferences are persisted but are **not enforced**; their Studio controls are disabled. `strictLocalOnly` does not block Chrome Sync or configured cloud integrations.
- `AUDIT_REPORT*.md`, `IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md`, and `OPTIMIZATION_REPORT.md` document historical investigations; their descriptions and recommendations may no longer match the implementation.
